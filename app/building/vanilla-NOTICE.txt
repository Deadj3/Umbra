vanilla.js: the game's building tile and furniture catalogs
============================================================
vanilla.js holds only the NAMES of tiles and how the game groups them (which tiles are the west and north piece of a wall, which tiles make a bed,
and so on). It contains no pictures and no part of Project Zomboid's art. The editor offers an entry only after the person has imported the
tilesheets it names from their own copy of the game.

The lists were read from BuildingTiles.txt and BuildingFurniture.txt of TileZed / BuildingEd (Tim Baker; maintained in the community project
"PZ Mapping Tools" by Unjammer, https://github.com/Unjammer/PZ_Mapping_Tools), which are distributed under the GNU General Public License v2.
Project Zomboid and its tiles belong to The Indie Stone. Umbra Editor is a free community mod tool, is not sold, and is not an official
The Indie Stone product. Regenerate with gen_vanilla.js when a newer catalog is wanted.
